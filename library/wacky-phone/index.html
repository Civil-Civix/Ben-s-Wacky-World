How a game unfolds

Every player starts a chain. Everyone writes, draws, or guesses at the same time, passing their page each round. A room of 6 plays 6 rounds, so every chain visits everyone.

Writing and guessing get 45 seconds; drawings get 90. Drafts save automatically. Mark yourself ready when finished, and keep editing until everybody is ready. The game advances immediately when all players are ready, or when the timer ends.

If someone disconnects, they have 30 seconds to return. The first disconnect each round adds 30 seconds to the shared timer. Missing pages are skipped. Your host reveals the finished stories one step at a time.

There are no points or votes. Rooms expire after 24 hours, so save your favorite chains before then.
